Rooms with a sense of place

The service

Interior design for homes in Santa Rosa, Healdsburg, Sonoma and across wine country, built around the pieces that give a home its character: furniture with history, made by hand and found abroad and close to home.

Every room begins with what is already there. We look at the pieces you love and the way you live, then source, place and balance new finds so the whole home reads as one considered place.

There are no packages. Each interiors commission is custom quoted and built around your budget.

  • Sourcing one-of-a-kind furniture and objects, foreign and domestic
  • Making new pieces work with the furniture you already own
  • A cohesive plan for each room and the home as a whole
