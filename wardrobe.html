A wardrobe, considered

The service

A wardrobe is a place too. Wardrobe styling, closet edits and personal sourcing in Santa Rosa and Sonoma County: we find clothing and accessories of real quality, abroad and close to home, and fit them to the pieces you already reach for.

We start with what you own and how you dress, then edit, source and arrange so every piece has a purpose and a place, and the wardrobe works as a whole.

There are no packages. Each wardrobe commission is custom quoted and built around your budget.

  • Closet edits: keeping, tailoring and letting go
  • Sourcing one-of-a-kind clothing and accessories, foreign and domestic
  • Making new pieces work with the wardrobe you already have
  • A cohesive plan for every season
