Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does an interior designer cost in Santa Rosa?

Every commission is custom quoted and built around your budget. There are no fixed packages: we learn about your home or wardrobe and what you want to achieve, then shape the scope to the investment you are comfortable with. Sharing an approximate range in your enquiry helps us propose the right approach.

Which areas do you serve?

The studio works from Santa Rosa across Sonoma County, including Healdsburg, Sonoma, Sebastopol, Petaluma, Windsor, Glen Ellen and Kenwood, and takes commissions across the greater Bay Area, including Napa, Marin and San Francisco.

What services does Topophilia Design Studio offer?

Interior design and wardrobe design. That covers sourcing one-of-a-kind furniture, clothing and accessories (The Collector), making new pieces work with what you already own (The Tailor), and building one cohesive plan for your rooms and wardrobe (The Builder).

Can you work with furniture and clothing I already own?

Yes. Every project starts with what is already there. We look at the pieces you love and set new finds alongside them, so your home and wardrobe feel collected rather than replaced.

Where do you source furniture and pieces?

We source high-quality, one-of-a-kind furniture, clothing and accessories both abroad and close to home, choosing each piece for the room or wardrobe it is going to.

Do you offer wardrobe styling and closet edits?

Yes. Wardrobe design includes closet edits (deciding what to keep, tailor and let go), sourcing one-of-a-kind clothing and accessories, and making new pieces work with the wardrobe you already have.

Do you design whole homes or single rooms?

Both. A commission can be a single room, a wardrobe, or a whole home, and each is quoted individually around your budget.

How do I start a project?

Fill in the enquiry form with a few details about your space, your wardrobe and your timeline. Your answers go directly to the studio, and we will be in touch personally to talk through your project and prepare a custom quote.
