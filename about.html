About the studio

A love of place

Topophilia Design offers interior design and wardrobe design services, sourcing high-quality, one-of-a-kind furniture, clothing and accessories from abroad and at home.

The mark

A carved horse, carrying finds home

Topophilia means a love of place. Our carved wooden horse carries finds home from near and far.

Two figures travel with it. One rides on its back and one stands beneath it, and each holds a long black iron bar in balance, the way a well-made room and a well-made wardrobe both depend on balance.
