Topophilia

Topophilia Design offers interior design and wardrobe design, sourcing high-quality, one-of-a-kind furniture, clothing and accessories from abroad and at home.

The mark

A carved horse, carrying finds home

Our carved wooden horse carries finds home from near and far. Two figures travel with it: one rides on its back and one stands beneath it.

Each holds a long black iron bar in balance, the way a well-made room and a well-made wardrobe both depend on balance.
