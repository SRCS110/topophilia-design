Topophilia

Topophilia Design Studio offers interior design and wardrobe design, sourcing high-quality, one-of-a-kind furniture, clothing and accessories from abroad and at home.

The studio works from Santa Rosa across Sonoma County, including Healdsburg, Sonoma and Sebastopol, and throughout the greater Bay Area.

The mark

A carved horse, carrying finds home

Our carved wooden horse carries finds home from near and far. Two figures travel with it: one rides on its back and one stands beneath it.

Each holds a long black iron bar in balance, the way a well-made room and a well-made wardrobe both depend on balance.

A home should feel like it could only be yours, and only be here.
